The Challenge of Scale

Distributed systems introduce complexity that simple applications never face. Consistency, availability, and partition tolerance often clash.

This maturity model provides a rubric to assess where your system stands, identifying gaps between "it works on my machine" and "it works at scale."

Maturity Domains

Key Pillars

Observability

Deep insight into system health via metrics, tracing, and structured logging.

Security

Zero-trust networking, identity management, and secrets rotation protocols.

Resilience

Graceful degradation, circuit breakers, and chaos engineering practices.

Scalability

Horizontal scaling capabilities, stateless services, and data sharding strategies.
